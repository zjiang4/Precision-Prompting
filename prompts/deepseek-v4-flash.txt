Mistake pattern:

How to avoid:
A hallmark phenotype outweighs a nondiagnostic initial test. Cat-like cry is a signature clue for a specific chromosomal syndrome; a normal routine karyotype should push you toward more targeted detection of a cryptic abnormality, not away from the diagnosis.

How to avoid:
