Historical-name mapping for this audit package
================================================

The raw sources and logs in this package were produced in June-July 2026 and
use the internal identifiers of that period. The mapping to the model names
used in the manuscript and in results/*.csv (as of 2026-09-11) is:

  V3                        -> MedSeek

  reasoning-medical0.1-27b  -> evaluated during development; NOT among the 14
                              models reported in the manuscript

as the published result files; only the identifiers differ.
